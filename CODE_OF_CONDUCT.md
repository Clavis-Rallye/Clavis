# Code of conduct

Clavis is built for people who are new to a city. We want the project around it to be just as
welcoming.

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
It applies in issues, pull requests, reviews and every other project space.

In short:

- Be respectful and patient, also when you disagree.
- Criticise ideas and code, never people.
- No harassment, no discrimination, no personal attacks.
- Respect the privacy of others. Do not publish anybody's personal data.

Report a problem to the maintainers at contact@clavis-rallye.de. Reports are handled in confidence. The
maintainers decide on the response, from a reminder to a ban from the project.
